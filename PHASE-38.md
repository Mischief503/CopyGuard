# Phase 38 — Token Research Assistant

CopyGuard v4.15.0 adds a dedicated Token Research Assistant over the evidence generated by Phase 25 and the deterministic Risk Engine v3 from Phase 26.

The assistant dossier covers SPL Token vs Token-2022 program identity, mint/freeze authorities, parsed Token-2022 extensions and restrictions, resolved holder concentration, creator-candidate holdings and launch history, creator funding evidence, same-ticker provenance, current market/liquidity evidence, Early Bird-to-holder overlap, local coordination, evidence coverage, explicit limitations, and risk-engine hard blocks/cautions/unknowns.

The assistant does not reconstruct evidence that CopyGuard has not observed. Historical liquidity removal remains unavailable when not evidenced, current market snapshots are not treated as historical execution evidence, creator identity remains a candidate unless proven, and UNKNOWN/UNAVAILABLE fields stay unknown.

Risk Engine v3 remains authoritative for PASS / CAUTION / HARD_BLOCK. The assistant cannot waive hard blocks, mark unknown evidence safe, change risk thresholds, or execute trades.
