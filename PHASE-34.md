# Phase 34 — CopyGuard System Awareness

CopyGuard v4.11.0 gives the governed assistant structured, read-only awareness of the complete evidence stack built in Phases 18–32.

The awareness envelope covers wallets, transaction identity, market pricing, Shadow state, verified outcomes, dynamic qualification, Early Bird reconstruction, token research, deterministic risk, wallet discovery, specialized AI audits, closed-loop learning, data integrity, connection health, and execution safety.

Evidence is query-targeted and bounded. Secrets/private credentials are scrubbed. UNKNOWN and UNAVAILABLE evidence must remain unknown. The assistant cannot mutate ledgers, settings, Trusted state, deterministic risk policy, or execution state through the awareness layer.
