3 / 7TRADE FEED
◉

Approving Trades

When a Pending wallet trades, it enters your approval queue. You decide whether to copy it.

1
Phase 12 Risk Score runs firstDeterministic checks: freeze/mint authority, liquidity, holder concentration, coordinated buys. Hard blocks cannot be overridden by AI.
2
AI gives a second opinionYour chosen AI provider analyzes the trade in context and returns COPY · CAUTION · SKIP with reasoning. Advisory only.
3
You approve or rejectApproved trades open in Padre for final confirmation. CopyGuard never places a trade without your direct action or an explicit Trusted rule.
⚡ Emergency Pause in the top toolbar instantly halts all Trusted automation with one click. The pause is active by default until you explicitly release it.
4 / 7COPYCAT DETECTION
⌕

Research — Avoid Copycats

On Solana there are often 4–5 fake versions of every coin that pumps. Buying the wrong one means losing everything.

1
Search any tickerGo to ⌕ Research and type BONK, WIF, or any ticker. CopyGuard pulls every Solana token sharing that name.
2
0–100 legitimacy scoreEach token is ranked by age, liquidity, DEX trust, and verified socials. The highest scorer is the likely original.
3
AI verdictYour AI provider gives a SAFE · CAUTION · DANGER verdict, copycat count, and specific advice. One click opens in Padre.
✦ The ✦ Early Bird tab also flags wallets that bought real tokens AND their copycats — spray-and-pray buyers are filtered out automatically.

Intelligence — Always Running

Three background engines run automatically. You don't need to do anything.

🎯
Wallet Discovery (every 30 min)Scans trending tokens on Solana. Surfaces wallets that appeared early on multiple big runs. One click adds them to your watchlist.
🏥
Health Monitor (every 5 min)Tracks rolling win rate of every Trusted wallet. Consecutive losses or a collapsed win rate triggers an alert — and optionally auto-demotes the wallet back to Pending.
✦
Early Bird Scan (every hour)Finds wallets with consistent early entry on tokens that ran 3x+. Scores timing, repeatability, run size, and contract precision. Spray-and-pray buyers are automatically filtered.
🔗 Bundle detection also runs on every trade: if two or more watched wallets buy the same token within 5 seconds, CopyGuard flags it as a possible coordinated pump before you copy in.
