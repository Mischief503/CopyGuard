# Phase 57 — Early Bird Intelligence UI

CopyGuard v4.34.0 turns Early Bird into a launch-reconstruction and source-evidence workspace.

## Evidence truth
- Earliest buyers are derived from actual inbound token-transfer evidence.
- Each retained event exposes entry rank, seconds after the reconstructed launch anchor, anchor source, reconstruction confidence, history completeness, source transaction signature, and historical entry-price evidence when available.
- Missing historical entry prices remain UNKNOWN.
- Observed run multiples remain DEX-window estimates and are never presented as verified historical peak prices.
- Source signatures link to Solscan.

## Wallet interpretation
- Repeatability is measured across independent token runs.
- Timing combines entry rank with actual time from the reconstructed launch anchor.
- Contract precision exposes copycat/spray behavior.
- Specialized AI is evidence-limited and cannot claim insider knowledge, proven profitability, or live eligibility.

## Routing boundary
- Strong Early Bird evidence may route a wallet into Ghost/Shadow testing.
- Early Bird never directly promotes a wallet to Trusted and never authorizes live execution.
- Shadow qualification remains a separate downstream requirement.
