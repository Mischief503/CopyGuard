# Phase 63 — Verified Wallet Performance Leaderboard

CopyGuard v4.40.0 replaces heuristic leaderboard ranking with an evidence-backed wallet board.

Authoritative performance uses only fully closed, source-verified Shadow outcomes. Open and partially realized Shadow lots remain qualification context and do not inflate verified realized ranking metrics.

The leaderboard exposes verified win rate, verified closes, verified net P&L, profit factor, chronological drawdown, independent-token repeatability, Early Bird/discovery context, deterministic risk decision counts, Shadow qualification state and current trust tier.

Ranking is informational only. It cannot promote a wallet to Trusted, authorize live execution, or waive deterministic risk.
