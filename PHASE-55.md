# Phase 55 — Research Center Evidence Workspace

CopyGuard v4.32.0 turns Research into an evidence-first token investigation workspace.

- Exact Solana mint resolution is visually separated from ticker/name comparison.
- Market quote, liquidity, FDV, market cap, volume and pair age remain current-market evidence.
- Phase 25 Evidence Package v3 is surfaced for token program, Token-2022 restrictions, mint/freeze authorities, owner-resolved concentration, creator candidate, creator holdings, related launches, funder evidence, early-holder overlap, coverage and limitations.
- Deterministic risk is displayed as PASS, CAUTION or HARD BLOCK with hard blocks, cautions and explicit UNKNOWN evidence.
- AI is visibly subordinate to deterministic risk and cannot mark a token safe or waive a block.
- Provenance is candidate evidence only; “likely original” is not identity proof.
- Padre handoff is review-only and never final-submits or creates accounting state.
- Hard-blocked contracts may still be opened for investigation, but the hard block remains visibly authoritative.
